Playback walkthrough
1. Open the app as a guest. Playback does not require account registration.
2. Open Channels and select a bundled demonstration channel. Source management also accepts https://bobtv.briconbric.com/api/v1/demo/demo.bobtv; this public demonstration service is deployed.
3. Inspect the import preview, confirm the categories/channels and select a channel. The demonstration contains licensed trailer loops and an original real-time test signal, delivered as continuously updated HLS. These are test channels with generated programme information.
4. On iPhone, the channel drawer opens from the bottom in portrait and from the right in landscape. iPad rearranges the player/channel layout by orientation. Try category filtering and channel switching.
